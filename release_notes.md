# StudySynth Education Version

### 🚀 Highlights
- **Target Exams**: Dedicated study workspace and test simulator for **UGC NET Education (Paper 2, Subject Code 09)** and **CTET Paper-II (Child Development & Pedagogy + Social Studies / Social Science)**.
- **8,519 Verified Questions**:
  - **UGC NET Education (Code 09)**: 2,024 questions classified across all 10 official NTA syllabus units.
  - **CTET Paper-II PYQs**: 6,495 questions consolidated and organized by subject & topic (CDP: Growth & Development, Learning Theories, Inclusive Ed, Assessment, Progressive Ed, Pedagogy; SST: History, Geography, Polity, Pedagogy).
  - Every question verified with correct answers, standard options, and clean non-PYQ metadata.
- **Realistic Exam Test Mode**:
  - **UGC NET Education (Paper 2) Mock**: 100 Qs | 120 Mins | 200 Marks (+2 / 0) — 10 questions evenly sampled across each of the 10 units.
  - **CTET Paper-II (SST + CDP) Mock**: 90 Qs | 90 Mins | 90 Marks (+1 / 0) — 30 CDP + 60 SST questions balanced across History, Geography, Polity, and Pedagogy.
  - **CTET CDP Section Mock**: 30 Qs | 30 Mins | 30 Marks (+1 / 0).
  - **Custom Test Builder**: Custom question counts (5 to 100) and time per question (30s to 120s).
- **True Randomization**: Automatic Fisher-Yates question order randomization on every Test attempt across all exam modes, while Practice mode maintains sequential order.
- **Zero PDF Bloat**: Unbloated package down to **25.2 MB** with zero PDF files.
- **100% Offline & Private**: Zero cloud dependencies, zero external tracking, on-device OCR and local storage.
