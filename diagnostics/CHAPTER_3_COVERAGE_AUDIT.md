## 1. Executive findings

**The chapter is not covered. It is sampled — thinly, and from a narrow slice of itself.**

- Reference inventory: **160 learning points** (102 Core, 51 Supporting, 6 Minor-reference, 1 REVIEW_REQUIRED).
- Question-worthy denominator: **143** (all Core + 41 Supporting with `question_suitable == yes`; 17 unsuitable entries excluded, 1 REVIEW_REQUIRED entry reported separately).
- Final coverage: **5 COVERED (3.5%) + 1 PARTIALLY_COVERED (0.7%) = 6 of 143 (4.2%)**. Core: 4 covered + 1 partial of 102 (4.9%). Supporting: 1 covered of 41 (2.4%). **137 question-worthy entries (95.8%) are UNCOVERED.**
- **7 of 13 question-worthy sections have zero coverage**: Introduction, Rock-Cut Architecture, Water Structures, Forts and Palaces, Tombs and Mausoleums, Churches in India, Colonial Architecture. The 6 covered-or-partial entries come from just 5 sections.
- **The current bank is not just thin — it contains an invalid question.** Q-P3-003 ("correct about the present structure?") has three defensible options: its distractors A (Indo-Saracenic architecture) and B (100,000 lights) are both also true of the Mysuru Palace, and its explanation falsely claims the source does not state them. It does not count as coverage for anything.
- **The 9 → 5 shrinkage is not mainly a quality purge.** Of the 9 previous questions: 5 were invalid-distractor items correctly blocked in form by the new same-section gate, 1 was a genuine extraction defect correctly removed, 2 survive (1 verbatim, 1 in improved form), and 1 (P2) has no named gate explaining its absence. The shrinkage from 9 to 5 is smaller than the real loss: the previous bank covered only a few more points than the current one, and both banks miss the same ~96% of the chapter.

**The loss is not one failure. It is a funnel with four choke points, in order of demonstrated impact:**

1. **Classification collapse (largest loss).** 149 of 179 extracted facts (83%) are `fallback`/unclassified sentence units. The candidate builders skip them outright, so 128 of 160 inventory entries (80%) exist in the fact layer only as unclassified text no typed builder can use.
2. **Selection pool exhaustion (second loss).** 47 candidates are built, 42 pass validation, but 37 validated candidates are discarded and the bank stops at 5 of a 15 target — without ever hitting the target cap (`target_limit: 0`). The discarded candidates recombine the same ~15 facts, and production metrics mislabel all 37 as `duplicate_relation` regardless of true cause (subtype cap, reused stem, or fill order are indistinguishable).
3. **Layout classification drops non-body content (third loss).** Table, caption, fragment, and ambiguous blocks are classified and then discarded by the batch (only `bodyText` is forwarded). The table-question path can therefore never fire on batch input; the timeline (book p. 87), the map (book pp. 88–89), and a caption-only page (book p. 122) yield zero facts; margin glosses (Gothic, Corbelled, Latticework) are corrupted or lost.
4. **Reasoning V2 contributes nothing (fourth loss).** 180 propositions are compiled, but 0 V2 candidates result — twice (internal run and standalone run). Fallback facts bridge into empty `HAS_PROPERTY` propositions, pairwise traversal caps at 100 pairs (propositions 101–179 never evaluated), and the constructor drops every vacuous candidate before validation, leaving V2 metrics empty.

**What is NOT the problem.** Page extraction is not failing: 40/40 pages are accepted. Validation is not the main killer: it rejects only 5 of 47 candidates. The previous bank's answers were not wrong: no previous key is incorrect. The engine is not losing a well-covered chapter to strict gates; it never represented most of the chapter in the first place.

## 2. Method and provenance

- **Independent extraction first.** The inventory was built from `pdftotext` (layout and default modes) over all 40 pages, then compared against the pipeline's own layout output — not derived from the pipeline's extractor, so the pipeline's blind spots did not become the inventory's blind spots.
- **Pipeline extraction caveat (honest).** The bundled `pwa/vendor/pdfjs/pdf.mjs` fails under Node in this environment (`a.toHex is not a function`), so the inventory worker classified blocks with the repo's unmodified `extractLayoutFromTextContent` fed by `pdfjs-dist` 4.2.67 (installed under `/tmp` only). Token text should match the browser path closely, but block classification must be re-confirmed on a browser run, especially book pp. 87, 98, 110. The Task 5 worker, by contrast, executed the repo's `extractPageLayout` path in Node and reproduced the production bank exactly; the two methods agree on the outcome (5 questions, same stems/subtypes/keys), but the classification caveat stands for the extraction matrix.
- **Importance is editorial, not inferred.** Core / Supporting / Minor-reference are provisional judgements with a recorded reason per entry. One entry (Fig. 3.2 timeline pairings, INV-3-007) is REVIEW_REQUIRED: neither `pdftotext` mode preserves the graphic's spatial pairing, and three timeline dates conflict with body text (Brihadisvara "around 1010" vs "1063 CE"; Ahmedabad mosque "15th century" vs "1424 CE"; Bishnupur "17th–18th" vs "17th–19th" centuries). Timeline dates must not be used as facts until visually confirmed.
- **Coverage definition (strict, and applied uniformly).** An entry is COVERED only when at least one final, valid question genuinely tests it. Mention in a distractor, mention in an explanation, and invalid questions (Q-P3-003) do not count. A question depending on another question or an unseen passage fails the context requirement; none of the 5 current questions does that, but Q-P3-001 is only PARTIALLY context-independent (stem names no temple) and Q-P3-005's stem ("correct about years?") is weak alone, rescued because every option is a full sentence carrying the design context.
- **What "question-worthy" excludes.** 17 entries are NOT_QUESTION_WORTHY (epigraph, framing questions, activities, figure-dependent items, explicitly-unknown source points). They are listed in the matrix with reasons and excluded from every denominator below. No single coverage percentage is reported without its denominator.

## 3. Task 1 — Actual pipeline trace

**Real call path (batch).** `pwa/tests/batch_generate_all_ncert.js:batchProcess()` loads vendored PDF.js, loops the 8-chapter manifest, and per page calls `extractPageLayoutText()` → `pdfParser.extractPageLayout()`, keeping only pages with `bodyText` longer than 30 chars and joining them with `==Page N==` markers. It then calls `analyze.analyzeSource()` (target 15), which chains `generator.parseRawDocument()` → `extractSourceFacts()` → `detectFactConflicts()` → `buildKnowledgeInventory()` → `generateQuestionBank()` (which internally runs the V2 engine) → `validateQuestionBank()`. The batch then runs `EngineV2.generateReasoningQuestionBank()` a second, standalone time (target 5, warning-only try/catch) and serialises Part I (production) + Part II (V2) via `buildMarkdownReport()` into the staged `.md` file and `summary_manifest.json`.

**Silent-loss points (content vanishes with no metric).** (1) Non-body blocks classified then dropped (475 blocks in Chapter 3: 57 table, 52 caption, 109 fragment, 27 ambiguous, 28 furniture; 371 excluded items counted in `stats`, then discarded). (2) Fallback facts bridge into empty V2 propositions and die in the constructor before validation, leaving V2 metrics empty. (5) All diagnostics are computed, then ignored by the batch; a thrown `NO_VERIFIED_QUESTIONS` becomes an ordinary 0-question bank and a V2 exception becomes one console warning. (6) `rejectionCounts` mixes fact-level counts (149 unsupported fallback facts) with candidate-level counts and sums to 217 against 42 rejected, so it cannot be read as a partition.

## 7. Task 5 — Instrumented diagnostic run (causal account, not a log file)

**Method.** A diagnostic wrapper (`diagnostics/tools/diagnose_chapter3.js`, self-contained apart from node + the repo) replicated the batch call path for Chapter 3 only: `pdfParser.extractPageLayout` per page (kept exactly as the batch keeps them: `bodyText.length > 30`), document assembly with the batch's header and `==Page N==` markers, `analyze.analyzeSource(raw, title, {sourceMeta}, 15)` (which internally runs parse → facts → conflicts → inventory → `generateQuestionBank` incl. internal V2 → validation), then the standalone V2 run (target 5). Output went to `/tmp` only; nothing was written to staging. The wrapper additionally called `buildQuestionCandidates` + per-candidate `validateQuestion` independently to recover numbers production metrics do not expose. One disclosed divergence: the wrapper's `buildQuestionCandidates` call passed `sourceMeta` singly-nested where production passes it doubly-nested; it had no observable effect here (candidate count and validation tallies match production exactly) but should be fixed if the harness is reused.

**Reproduction.** The run reproduces the production bank exactly: 5/5 stems with the same subtype and answer key (entity_attribute 1, multi_statement 1, statement_option 2, assertion_reason 1). A second identical `analyzeSource` run was byte-identical on stem/subtype/key/option signatures: the pipeline is deterministic on this input, so any future non-reproduction means code or input drift, not randomness. Standalone V2 also yielded 0, matching the bank's 0 V2 items.

**Causal account of the loss (trace-supported only).** (1) Classification: 149 fallback facts are skipped by the builder's first loop (`unsupported_facts: 149`); this alone removes 83% of extracted content before any question logic runs. (2) Selection: 37 of 42 validated candidates are discarded while the bank sits at 5 of 15 — the multi/AR builders recombine the same tiny fact pool (e.g. F-LITE-025 "Stupas … common in Jainism too" appears in 19 candidates), so relation/stem reuse exhausts the selectable pool; the 40% subtype cap and fill order may contribute but cannot be separated in production metrics. (3) Whole forms produce zero candidates despite facts existing: all 5 membership, both causal, and the single comparison fact yield no candidate, plus 6 entity_attribute and 1 numeric fact (direct-maker null returns total `missingOptions: 25`). (4) V2 adds no rescue: 180 propositions, 0 candidates. Validation, by contrast, is a minor loss (5 candidates), and implied post-rotation rejections are 0 (5 validation failures + 0 post-rotation = the 5 non-build, non-selection rule counts; the arithmetic closes exactly).

**What could NOT be measured (explicit).** Per-fact builder failure reason (builder exposes only aggregate `buildMetrics`; `missingOptions: 25` counts every direct-maker null return — missing distractors or any other maker failure, undistinguished — so there is no clean "rejected for lack of valid distractors" count; distractor-rule validation issues are separately 0 here). Which fact is the single `unsafeFacts: 1` skip (`isFactUnsafe` is not exported). Facts the multi/multi-3/AR/matching/chronology/table builders considered and discarded (they return only survivors). Pre-validation V2 candidates by subtype or per-candidate reasons (V2 produced 0 candidates, so there is nothing to break down). Separate counts for dedup vs relationship reuse vs subtype-cap vs target-cap (all inside the private selection loop; reported as the single production-labelled `duplicate_relation` bucket of 37). Why any individual sentence was classified fallback (the trace shows the classification, not the classifier's reason). No missing question is attributed to a specific rejection beyond what the trace above supports.

## 9. Root causes, ranked by demonstrated impact

1. **Fact classification collapses 83% of extracted content to unclassified units.** 149/179 facts are `fallback`; the builder skips them; 128/160 inventory entries are FALLBACK_ONLY. No downstream stage — distractors, V2, validation, selection — can recover content that was never classified. This is the audit's largest single loss, and it is a representation failure, not a strictness failure.
2. **Selection exhausts a tiny pool and hides why.** 37 of 42 validated candidates are discarded while the bank sits at 5/15; the multi/AR builders recombine the same ~15 facts; production metrics collapse every cause into `duplicate_relation`. The bank is small because the selectable pool is small, not because the target was hit.
3. **Layout classification discards non-body blocks, then the batch discards the evidence.** Table/caption/fragment/ambiguous blocks never reach fact extraction; per-page `stats` recording the exclusion are computed and thrown away. The table-question path, timeline, map, glosses, and caption-subject pairs are all casualties of this one decision.
4. **Sectioning by page flush breaks the section signal the gates rely on.** 43 sections are mostly page slices sharing one repeated title. Same-section ordering and the same-section gate therefore operate on near-meaningless sections — which is why C1 passes the gate with only 1/3 same-section distractors and why "same-section" cannot separate a national park from map concepts in Chapter 1 either.
5. **V2 runs on empty propositions and reports nothing.** Fallback facts become empty `HAS_PROPERTY` propositions; pairwise traversal caps at 100 pairs; the constructor drops vacuous candidates pre-validation. V2's 0-for-Chapter-3 is structural, not a threshold that can be tuned.
6. **Validation does not check option truth.** Q-P3-003 passes validation with three defensible options because validation checks the answer against its excerpt, not each option against the source. Statement-option distractors built from other facts' values are never verified false *of this subject in this context*.
7. **Sentence handling loses co-sentence facts silently.** Early returns in `parseFactRelations` and uncounted `continue` skips mean one sentence yields at most one fact type; 368 units → 179 facts with no per-unit log, so no one can say which sentence lost what.
8. **All diagnostics are computed and then ignored.** Generation metrics, validation objects, conflicts, inventory tiers, per-page stats, and unused/conflicted fact ids exist in memory during the run and are written nowhere. Every failure above was therefore invisible to the batch operator; the only visible numbers were page counts and question counts.

## 10. Task 7 — Recommended architecture changes (evidence-supported only)

Changes are ordered as specified. Each states the observed problem, its evidence, the responsible module or missing component, the proposed deterministic solution, how it will be tested, and what it cannot solve without additional capabilities. No fixed per-section, per-fact, or per-chapter question quota is recommended anywhere: quotas do not measure coverage.

### 1. Source extraction and reconstruction

- **Problem.** Non-body blocks are classified then dropped; furniture merges into sentences; cross-page sentences are never stitched; captions detach from subjects.
- **Evidence.** §5 failure modes (1)–(4); 3 zero-fact pages; timeline/map/gloss losses; `stats` discarded (§3, Stage 1).
- **Module.** `pwa/engine/pdfParser.js` (`extractLayoutFromTextContent`, `extractPageLayout`) + `batch_generate_all_ncert.js:extractPageLayoutText` (forwards only `bodyText`).
- **Solution.** Forward all classified blocks with their types into the pipeline instead of only `bodyText`; strip running furniture by position/repetition *before* sentence segmentation, with a rule that never splits a sentence mid-clause; stitch sentence continuations across page breaks using terminal-punctuation + lowercase-continuation heuristics already present in extraction gates; reattach caption heads to the nearest body subject on the same page (caption blocks carry figure numbers that body text references); capture table/list blocks as structured rows with their header relationships instead of prose. Emit per-page block counts and per-block dispositions in batch output.
- **Test.** Re-run the Chapter 3 diagnostic: block-disposition counts must account for 100% of blocks; book pp. 87, 88–89, 98, 106, 110, 122 must improve from NOT_EXTRACTED/SEVERELY INCOMPLETE or carry an explicit VISUAL_ONLY reason; no previously FAITHFUL page may regress (compare per-page verdicts).
- **Cannot solve.** True visual content (timeline pairings, map positions, plan layouts, image-only labels) cannot be recovered from the text layer by any deterministic text rule; those items stay VISUAL_ONLY with an explicit reason, and any future use needs a visual-reading step outside this design.

### 2. Knowledge representation

- **Problem.** Flat subject–relation–object facts with empty subjects/objects cannot represent the chapter's relationships, and fallback units masquerade as facts.
- **Evidence.** 149 fallback facts with empty subject/object; 11 typed facts corrupted (fragment/pronoun/figure-ref subjects, truncated objects); V2's 176 empty propositions; inventory relationships (temple arrangement, mosque composition, fort layout, tomb symmetry) that have no single-triple form.
- **Module.** `pwa/engine/generator.js` (`extractSourceFacts`, `normalizeSubject/Relation/Object`, `buildFactIndex`) + `pwa/engine/reasoning/representation.js` (`mapFactToProposition`).
- **Solution.** Extend the fact record with: raw subject/object spans alongside normalised ones; qualifier fields (negation, approximators, units, dates, direction); source span (page, section, sentence id); evidence quote; and a `classification_reason` for every unparsed unit. Stop emitting empty fallback facts: keep unparsed units in a separate `unparsed_units` list with a reason code, so they are visible and countable instead of poisoning the graph. Restrict V2 graph input to typed facts only. Add structured forms the chapter demonstrably needs: composition/part-of (temple elements, mosque features, fort contents), arrangement (alignment, plan order), and attribution (builder, dynasty, period) — each with the same evidence discipline as existing relations.
- **Test.** Chapter 3 diagnostic: fallback share must fall with a per-reason breakdown; zero empty propositions in the V2 graph; every typed fact must carry a non-empty subject and object or be rejected with a reason; the 11 corrupted typed facts must either be repaired or reclassified with reasons.
- **Cannot solve.** Importance judgement and implicit meaning: no representation change makes a deterministic system recognise that an unstated implication is exam-worthy. That judgement stays human (gold-standard inventory) until proven otherwise.

### 3. Coverage planning

- **Problem.** There is no inventory and no planner: generation starts from facts and stops at a count target, so uncovered content is invisible.
- **Evidence.** 137/143 question-worthy entries uncovered with no output anywhere naming them; bank stops at 5/15 for pool reasons, not coverage reasons.
- **Module.** Missing component (partially: `buildKnowledgeInventory` exists but its tiers are never printed or used for planning).
- **Solution.** Make the inventory a first-class pipeline product: every run emits an inventory (learning points with evidence, type, section, page) and a coverage matrix against it, before question construction. Add a planner that orders learning points (Core first, then Supporting) and assigns builders by knowledge type; builders work from the planner's list, not from raw fact iteration order. The uncovered-Core list is a required output of every run, alongside (never instead of) the bank.
- **Test.** Chapter 3 diagnostic: planner output must list all 102 Core entries with a disposition (covered / attempted / no-builder / invalid); re-running the audit against the same reference inventory must reproduce the §8 numbers exactly before any builder change.
- **Cannot solve.** The planner cannot invent importance: its ordering needs either the gold-standard inventory or a documented structural heuristic (headings, definitions, named entities) whose errors are measured, not assumed away.

### 4. Deterministic question construction

- **Problem.** Whole knowledge types produce zero candidates despite facts existing, and direct-form failure drops the fact instead of trying another form.
- **Evidence.** All 5 membership, both causal, and the single comparison fact yield 0 candidates; 6 entity_attribute + 1 numeric facts also yield none (`missingOptions: 25` aggregate, undistinguished).
- **Module.** `pwa/engine/generator.js` makers (`makeMembershipCandidate`, `makeCauseCandidate`, `makeComparisonCandidate`, etc.) + `buildQuestionCandidates`.
- **Solution (two steps, in order).** First, instrument maker null reasons (a diagnostic-only counter per maker per reason: no-distractors vs subject gate vs stem gate vs other) — the current aggregate cannot attribute any single loss, and redesigning builders before measuring would repeat the audit's central mistake. Second, guided by those reasons: fix the specific failing preconditions, and add form fallback — if a direct form fails, try the statement-option form (mutated sentence, on-topic by construction) before dropping the fact, as the current bank already demonstrates for facts that survive in statement-option form.
- **Test.** Chapter 3 diagnostic: maker-reason breakdown must account for all 25 `missingOptions`; membership/causal/comparison facts must each gain a candidate or a named reason; no previously valid question may be lost (bank diff + audit re-run).
- **Cannot solve.** Plausible distractors for every possible question: where no same-section, same-type alternative exists, the correct outcome remains dropping the question (as the entity gate already does), not inventing an option.

### 5. Context assembly

- **Problem.** Questions can ship without the base a student needs: stems that name no subject or dimension.
- **Evidence.** Q-P3-001 ("correct about the main alignment?" — no temple named); Q-P3-005 ("correct about years?" — subject only in options); P3's "the present structure" (no palace named). The audit's context rule already exists in prose but not as a check.
- **Module.** Missing component (partially: `buildStemContext` exists but assembles explanation context, not a question contract).
- **Solution.** Adopt the question contract as data, not documentation: every candidate carries `context` (the minimum background: named subject + dimension + any disambiguating scope), `stem`, `answer`, `evidence`, `explanation`, `distractors`, and `coverage_links` (inventory ids). Add a deterministic self-containment check: the stem + options (or stem + context field, once the app renders it) must name the subject and dimension with no unresolved pronoun and no dependence on another question; failures are rewritten from available context (subject from the fact, dimension from the relation) or rejected with a reason. The context must not reveal the answer (it must not contain the answer value or a paraphrase that gives it away).
- **Test.** Chapter 3 bank re-audit: Q-P3-001-style stems must either gain their subject or be rejected; a regression set of context-free stems (including "What measurement is stated for north?") must all fail the check; no valid question's context may contain its answer value (automated scan).
- **Cannot solve.** Whether a context sentence is *pedagogically* sufficient for a student is a human judgement; the check guarantees the referents are present, not that the question is well taught. The context-field vs context-in-stem rendering decision is still open (see §12) and must be settled before this is built.

### 6. Answer and distractor validation

- **Problem.** Validation checks the answer against its excerpt but never checks each option against the source, so multi-correct items pass.
- **Evidence.** Q-P3-003 passes with three defensible options; its explanation falsely claims the source does not state A/B. Same-section distractor truth is likewise unchecked.
- **Module.** `pwa/engine/generator.js` (`validateQuestion`, `validateQuestionBank`) + `pwa/engine/reasoning/validator.js`.
- **Solution.** Add an option-truth check for statement-option (and multi-statement) items: every option is checked against the source graph/facts for the same subject — an option that is true of the subject in the source is rejected as a distractor, not merely noted. Separate fact-level from candidate-level rejection accounting so `rejectionCounts` partitions (no more 217-against-42). Record a per-candidate selection-loss reason (cap vs dedup vs subtype-cap vs fill order) instead of the blanket `duplicate_relation` label.
- **Test.** Q-P3-003 must fail validation under the new check (A and B true of Mysuru Palace); all 5 current valid questions must still pass; rejection accounting must sum exactly to rejected counts in the Chapter 3 diagnostic.
- **Cannot solve.** Truth checking is only as good as the fact layer: where extraction or classification failed, an option can be wrongly judged false (or true) — validation improvements do not repair §10.1–10.2 failures, and must not be used to excuse them.

### 7. Coverage-gap closure

- **Problem.** Uncovered content has no second chance: one generation pass, one selection, no targeted retry.
- **Evidence.** 137 uncovered question-worthy entries; the bank stops at 5 while 42 validated candidates were discarded for pool reasons, with no retry for the uncovered majority.
- **Module.** Missing component (selection loop in `generateQuestionBank`).
- **Solution.** After selection, run a gap-closure pass over the uncovered-Core list only: for each uncovered Core entry, attempt the remaining applicable builders/forms (including statement-option and multi-statement forms that recombine already-typed facts) and record a per-entry outcome (covered / attempted-with-reason / no-valid-form). Do not lower any validation bar in this pass; an entry that cannot yield a valid question gets an explicit NOT_COVERED_WITH_REASON entry, and the run ends with that list, not with a padded bank.
- **Test.** Chapter 3 diagnostic: gap-closure pass must reduce uncovered Core or increase the explicit-reason list by the same amount (no silent change); uncovered-Core count is reported before and after the pass; no invalid question may enter through this pass (re-audit the final bank).
- **Cannot solve.** Entries whose only valid form does not exist in the builder set (e.g. visual-only, timeline pairings) stay uncovered with reasons; gap closure cannot create a form the engine does not have.

### 8. Final coverage audit

- **Problem.** Banks ship with counts, not coverage, so regressions are invisible: 9 → 5 looked like a quality purge and was mostly a pool accident.
- **Evidence.** Every metric in §7–§8 existed in memory and was written nowhere; the 9→5 comparison required reconstructing a previous bank from a clone.
- **Module.** Missing component (batch serialisation in `batch_generate_all_ncert.js`).
- **Solution.** Ship the coverage matrix with every bank: bank headers report coverage by importance (covered/partial/uncovered/denominator) alongside counts, and the matrix JSON is versioned with the bank. Add a coverage regression check to the test suite: regenerating Chapter 3 against this audit's reference inventory must not reduce Core coverage or increase invalid questions without an explicit, reviewed reason. Keep the reference inventory stable across versions so future audits compare like with like.
- **Test.** A deliberate regression (e.g. disabling the entity gate) must be caught by the coverage check; the Chapter 3 matrix must reproduce §8 numbers exactly from the shipped bank + inventory.
- **Cannot solve.** The audit guarantees traceability, not quality: a bank can have perfect matrix hygiene and still contain awkward or unpedagogical questions. Human reading of the bank remains necessary.

## 11. Known limitations

- **Browser re-confirmation outstanding.** Block classification in the extraction matrix was produced with the repo's layout function fed by `pdfjs-dist` 4.2.67 under Node, because the vendored pdf.js fails under Node here. Token text should match the browser path, but classification on book pp. 87, 98, 110 (and furniture handling generally) must be re-confirmed on a browser run before Phase 2 treats those verdicts as final.
- **Section identity is inferred, not engine-native.** Same-section judgements in the question audit use PDF headings/paragraphs, not engine `section_id` values (absent from banks). No 0/3 distractor set has a plausible same-section member under any heading-based reading, but borderline pairs could shift under the engine's page-slice sectioning.
- **P2's absence is unexplained.** No named gate explains why previous Q-P3-002's form vanished while a different multi item on related facts survived. Do not cite P2 as evidence for any specific rule until a per-candidate trace exists.
- **Importance is provisional.** Core/Supporting/Minor judgements are editorial and chapter-specific. They are a measuring instrument, not a discovery about the textbook. A different reader could move entries between Core and Supporting; the coverage percentages would move with them. The funnel (representation → candidate → selection) is robust to those moves; the exact percentages are not.
- **One chapter, one source, one engine version.** Nothing here generalises by itself to other chapters, subjects, or source types. Chapter 3 is architecture-heavy (visual, spatial, caption-dependent); a history or civics chapter will fail differently.
- **Inventory granularity.** At least one entry (INV-3-067) merges two distinct facts (tower + alignment) into one row, which is why it is PARTIALLY_COVERED rather than cleanly covered/uncovered. Future inventories should split such entries; the matrix schema supports it (one row per learning point, and a question may cover more than one).
- **What was not examined.** Browser-path behaviour (worker/jobs/app one-step import, OCR review stop), other chapters, the app's rendering of questions, and the consolidated `ALL_NCERT_CLASS_8_QUESTIONS.md` file (a copy of the banks, not a separate source) were out of scope.

## 12. Recommended next implementation phase (for review — do not start yet)

**Phase 2, first slice: make coverage visible and repair representation before touching question construction.** Concretely: (a) land the inventory + coverage matrix as pipeline outputs (recommendation 3 + 8, diagnostic harness first, no builder changes); (b) repair extraction forwarding and furniture/caption handling (recommendation 1) with the Chapter 3 per-page verdicts as the regression test; (c) land the typed-fact record with qualifiers, spans, and `unparsed_units` with reason codes, and restrict the V2 graph to typed facts (recommendation 2). Only after (a)–(c) are measured against this audit's reference inventory should builder work (recommendation 4, starting with maker-reason instrumentation) begin.

**Two decisions needed from review before Phase 2 starts.** (1) Context rendering: context as a separate question field shown above the stem, or context baked into the stem as a leading sentence? The audit's contract supports either; the app and the bank format must pick one. (2) Gold-standard ownership: is the reference inventory maintained by human review (recommended — it is the only honest importance measure), or derived by structural heuristic with measured error? Phase 2's coverage claims depend on this choice.

**Explicitly not recommended now.** Distractor tuning in isolation (the pool is too small for tuning to matter); question-count targets or per-section quotas (they do not measure coverage); restoring previous-bank questions (5 of 9 were invalid-distractor items; the 2 retained already survive); and any LLM runtime dependency (nothing in this audit requires one — the failures are extraction, representation, and accounting, not language understanding beyond deterministic rules).
